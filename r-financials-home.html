Capabilities

What R Financials owns for you.

Decision-useful analysis, not spreadsheets. Every engagement moves from data, to analysis, to judgment, to recommendation.

01

Financial Modeling

Three-statement, operating, DCF, LBO and acquisition models built as decision-making tools, with scenarios, sensitivities and returns.

02

Investment Analysis

Underwriting, investment memoranda, return and risk analysis, upside/downside cases and portfolio monitoring, driven to a clear recommendation.

03

Valuation & Transactions

DCF, trading and transaction comparables, precedent analysis, accretion/dilution, entry/exit multiples and transaction structuring.

04

Due Diligence

Quality-of-earnings, margin and working-capital analysis, revenue and concentration review, key risks and deal-breaker identification.

05

Strategic Finance

FP&A, forecasting, profitability and KPI analysis, capital allocation and scenario planning for major decisions.

06

Transaction Support

Acquisitions, sell-side processes and capital raises, spanning buyer/investor analysis, CIM development, transaction models and execution.
